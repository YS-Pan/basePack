basePack's runtime.py adapts the model wrapper, text/image conversation template
and last-unmasked-token pooling. It removes video and remote/CPU loading, uses
strict local image/token validation and emits float32 L2-normalized embeddings.
The runtime and strict preprocessing also reuse this project's earlier local
VL implementation. Model weights and installed dependencies remain external.
